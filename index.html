02 / QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked
questions.

How do I play?

Tap anywhere on the playfield when the moving cyan ball is inside the magenta target zone. The run is already moving when the game opens.

How does the difficulty increase?

Each successful hit adds one to your streak, speeds up the ball, and makes the target gap smaller. A perfect hit is recognized near the center of the gap and gets extra visual feedback.

How do I mute the game?

Tap the speaker icon in the upper-right corner. It turns off synthesized game sounds; haptic feedback is controlled separately by iOS.

How do I restart after missing?

A miss ends the current run. Tap the playfield once to start again immediately—there is no menu or countdown.

Where are my records saved?

Your highest streak is saved in the app’s local iOS preferences on your device. GAPSTRIKE does not send the score to a developer server or provide an online leaderboard.

Does the game require an internet connection?

No. The game works offline and does not need an internet connection to play.
