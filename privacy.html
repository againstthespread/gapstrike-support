GAPSTRIKE / PRIVACY

A clear view of
your data.

GAPSTRIKE keeps your best streak and sound choice in the app on your device. The game does not send them to a developer server.

Effective date: November 17, 2026

01 / ON YOUR DEVICE

What GAPSTRIKE stores

The app saves two values in its local iOS preferences using UserDefaults:

  • Best streak: the highest streak you have reached.
  • Sound preference: whether synthesized game sounds are enabled.

The game reads touch input while you play, but it does not store a tap history or send gameplay events to a developer service. The saved streak is not attached to an account because GAPSTRIKE has no account system.

02 / COLLECTION

Personal information

The current GAPSTRIKE app does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer or third parties. Its source contains no network client, account service, online leaderboard, or cloud-sync feature. It does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone input.

The app works offline. Its local score and sound preference remain in app data on the device and are not available to the developer through the game.

03 / DEVICE BACKUPS

Apple device backups

If iCloud Backup is enabled, Apple may include app data in a device backup according to your device’s backup settings. GAPSTRIKE does not use iCloud Drive or iCloud syncing, and the developer does not receive or access a device backup. Backup and restore controls are provided by Apple. Learn what iCloud Backup can include.

04 / SERVICES

Third parties, analytics, and ads

GAPSTRIKE includes no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, advertising, tracking scripts, or developer-operated servers. It uses Apple frameworks for the game, synthesized audio, haptics, and local preferences.

This privacy policy covers the GAPSTRIKE app. Apple’s own operating-system, App Store, and optional device-backup services are governed by Apple’s terms and privacy information.

05 / RETENTION

Retention and deletion

The best streak and sound preference remain with the app’s local data while iOS retains that data. GAPSTRIKE has no developer-side account or server copy. You can remove the local app data by deleting GAPSTRIKE and its data through iOS. If you restore the device from a backup that contains the app data, those saved values may return.

If you choose to email support, your email address and whatever information you include are delivered to the support mailbox by your email service and used to respond. Support correspondence remains in that mailbox until deleted; the email provider may retain backups under its own settings and policies. Please do not include passwords or other sensitive information.

06 / CHILDREN

Children’s privacy

GAPSTRIKE does not ask players for a name, age, account, or other personal information, and it has no chat or user-generated-content features. The game stores only the local gameplay preference and best streak described above. If a parent or guardian has a privacy question, please contact support.

07 / UPDATES

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this page will be updated to describe them. The effective date at the top will change when the policy is revised.
